## iteration

* [Iteration 10: the tab-mcp extension's AI app card](iteration-10-ai-card.md) - The side panel's plain pairing view gives way to the tab-mcp extension's AI app card (DEC-017): an AI access switch, Turn on AI access, the address and code at once with no app paired, + Add another AI app, Disconnect asking first; a panel covered by another extension's panel hands its questions to notifications.
* [Iteration 11: Always allow for AI apps](iteration-11-always-allow.md) - Always allow comes back (DEC-018): per AI app and tool from a question in the side panel or a notification, and Allow all changes per app in the AI app card, with Ask again for each tool; kept in the worker's settings database until the app is disconnected.
* [Iteration 12: AI findings on marks, and the summary for the developer](iteration-12-findings.md) - An AI app adds findings of its own to a mark, apart from the user's comment, and edits or deletes only its own (by its client id); the user deletes any in the side panel; the case's Verdict becomes the Summary for the developer, set_verdict becomes set_summary, the stored field stays verdict (DEC-019).
* [Iteration 13: setup steps per AI app in the pairing box](iteration-13-setup-steps.md) - Client 0.12.1 and relay 0.9.0; the pairing box gets a collapsed "How do I add it?" with the client library's steps per AI app (Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex), each command with its own Copy, the guide link last; the questions escape with the library's escapeInvisible.
* [Iteration 14: Export says where the file went, Export all cases, tooltips](iteration-14-export.md) - Export shows the saved file's path with Copy and Show in folder, through the optional downloads permission asked at the first Export (a no saves as before); Export all cases writes one zip with a folder per case, which Import a case… reads case by case; the side panel's buttons get tooltips (DEC-020).
* [Iteration 15: one-way sync of the cases to a folder](iteration-15-sync.md) - Sync to a folder…: the user picks a folder once (File System Access API) and the service worker writes each case there as its single-case export, again whenever it changes; a deleted case keeps its file, nothing is read back; Chrome's permission lasts only while the side panel is open, so the panel shows Sync paused with Allow access, and changes made meanwhile are written then (DEC-021).
* [Iteration 16: opening a case shows its marks on the tab in front](iteration-16-auto-show.md) - Opening a case draws its marks on the tab in front, whatever page that is, and they follow the tab in front while the case is open (another tab, a reload, a navigation); All cases takes them off, a Hide holds on its tab; a case with none of its marks found says it is probably for another page; without access to the tab a notice with a warning sign and Allow on all sites, and only what needs the page is off (DEC-022).
* [Iteration 17: one coherent flow for tester, developer and tester again; a new look; Verify; sync without missed prompts](iteration-17-ux-rework.md) - Overnight plan of 2026-11-12/13 (the owner): a Fable UX review of the whole extension from the three roles, then a reworked flow (marking only inside an open case; All cases closes it), a new visual design with real contrast, a Verify step for the tester, and folder sync that does not depend on a missed Allow click (a Downloads subfolder as default, any folder as an option).
* [Iteration 18: a Firefox build and its release](iteration-18-firefox.md) - Sitegloss in desktop Firefox from one codebase (an event page, a sidebar, Firefox's data collection consent for AI access, sync to Downloads), and a release that publishes itself to addons.mozilla.org with a source archive (DEC-034, DEC-035).
* [Iteration 1: skeleton](iteration-1-skeleton.md) - The repository's tooling, a build that produces the unpacked extension with a fixed ID, the service worker that opens the side panel and injects the content script on a click, icons, CI, and an end-to-end harness that loads the extension into Chromium.
* [Iteration 2: mark and save](iteration-2-mark.md) - Drawing tools on the page (element, box, arrow, pen, note) with a comment each, element details, and saving a named case with screenshots with their marks and crops into IndexedDB; the side panel lists and shows cases.
* [Iteration 3: export, import and loading onto another build](iteration-3-reload.md) - Export a case as a zip with case.json, case.md and WebP images; import it; load a case onto the tab in front, find each mark's element by a weighted similarity score, and highlight it with its confidence.
* [Iteration 4: AI apps through tab-mcp](iteration-4-mcp.md) - TabMcp in the side panel with a relay setting and the connect dialog, and the tools list_cases, get_case, get_image, load_case, highlight, capture, add_mark, set_verdict and delete_case.
* [Iteration 5: pilot and store](iteration-5-pilot.md) - A first pilot with a PO and a dev on the pages of a real site, measuring how often elements are found; the privacy policy and the store listing; the extension unlisted in the Chrome Web Store and the store's key in the manifest.
* [Iteration 6: trial on dev pages and the refactor](iteration-6-trial-refactor.md) - Overmark on the dev pages of a real site (matching measured, a scale bug found and fixed), then three refactors the reviews asked for: an allow-list of recorded details, one state machine per page, and a side panel drawn from one state.
* [Iteration 7: full review](iteration-7-full-review.md) - A review of the whole codebase by four Claude reviewers and GLM: 70 findings, 57 distinct. Screenshots moved to the moment a mark is drawn, which removed the cause of the worst ones; the rest fixed or answered.
* [Iteration 8: the AI connection without the side panel](iteration-8-worker-connection.md) - The connection to the AI app moves from the side panel into the service worker (DEC-015), so it stays while the panel is closed and comes back after a restart; the panel becomes a view with a plain pairing view of its own; a tool that changes something asks in the side panel of the window in front, else in a Chrome notification.
* [Iteration 9: the AI settings in the worker's IndexedDB](iteration-9-settings-idb.md) - The address secret and the AI settings move from chrome.storage.local, which content scripts may reach, into an IndexedDB database the service worker alone uses (DEC-016); nothing is taken from chrome.storage.local, only deleted; the panel's localStorage handover stays, in one transaction; the side panel changes the settings only over its port; setAccessLevel best effort; Chrome 116 again.
